The Claim

"The network just routes packets."

"The orchestrator just routes tasks."

The Leak

Networks accumulated DNS, DHCP, NAT, ACLs, service discovery, observability, identity, and policy — until "just routing packets" described a small fraction of operational reality.

Agentic systems are already accumulating capability discovery, context propagation, memory, approval gates, tracing, identity, retries, and governance. Same trajectory.

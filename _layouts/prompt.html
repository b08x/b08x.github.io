How it works

Move a dial and the pipeline re-runs: the prompt's clauses are re-picked, the ground is re-checked, and whatever you export is cut from that result.

Ground: invariant

The dials change how these propositions are realized, never which propositions are made. Each anchor is re-checked against the generated text after every adjustment.
